## Feature Requests

Good feature requests explain:

- The workflow you are trying to complete
- What currently gets in the way
- What a successful experience would look like
- Any alternatives or workarounds you have tried

## Bug Reports

Good bug reports include:

- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Your operating system
- The `hob` version or commit
- Relevant logs, screenshots, or recordings

## Product Copy

The product name is always written lowercase as `hob`.
